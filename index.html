Calibration details

The fit approximates your LUT using a tone curve, Color Blender and Color Grading. It uses provisional +1.207630378 EV normalization. Do not add this as camera exposure compensation.

Errors estimate synthetic color-cell responses. Every generated preset is a model prediction and should be checked in NX Studio. Targets outside model coverage receive a warning.
